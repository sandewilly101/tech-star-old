Events

Our in-school STEM and IoT – Robotics workshops give students hands-on digital literacy skills: coding, IoT and robotics that build problem-solving, logical thinking and 21st-century skills, aligned with the school curriculum.

8th & 9th March 2025 Buni Hub, COSTECH, Dar es Salaam

Empowering Future Innovators: International Women's Day 2025 & TeensInAI Hackathon

Join us on March 8th and 9th, 2025, at Buni Hub, COSTECH Building, for an extraordinary celebration of International Women's Day 2025 in collaboration with TeensInAI under TechStar Innovation Hub. This two-day event brings together young minds, especially girls, to explore the transformative power of Artificial Intelligence (AI), coding, and entrepreneurship while addressing real-world challenges through technology-driven solutions.

24 March 2025 Mtwara Technical High School
Arduino Day 2025: Celebrating Innovation & Creativity with TechStar Innovation Hub

Get ready for an electrifying celebration of technology and creativity at Arduino Day 2025, organized by TechStar Innovation Hub this March at Mtwara Technical High School! Arduino Day is a global event that brings together makers, innovators, educators, and tech enthusiasts to explore the endless possibilities of Arduino — the open-source electronics platform that powers creativity, innovation, and hands-on learning. This year, TechStar Innovation Hub is taking it a step further by creating a dynamic space for students, developers, engineers, and tech lovers to connect, learn, and build.
